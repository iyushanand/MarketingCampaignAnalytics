BI Integration Guide
Connecting Tableau to the CSV Data Source:
  1. Click **Export All** to generate the CSV datasets in the project archive.
  2. Open **Tableau Desktop** or **Tableau Public** on your machine.
  3. Under Connect -> To a File, select **Text File**.
  4. Navigate to the directory Tableau/Datasets/ inside the project workspace and load `campaign_performance.csv`.
  5. Connect other files like `customer_analytics.csv` and join them using the primary keys (e.g. customer demographics mapping) where required.
Executive Dashboard Metrics:
  • **Overview Sheet**: Display sum of Revenue, sum of Spend, and calculated Portfolio ROI.
  • **Campaign Performance**: Stacked bar chart detailing Spend vs Revenue.
  • **Demographics**: Geographical map using Country fields joined with customer spend indices.
